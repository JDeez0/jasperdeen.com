The dune crest line across the top of this page is generated procedurally:
seeded randomness produces a consistent field of crescent dunes — lit
windward ramps, shaded slip faces, and tapered contour ribbons — at any
width, with zero JavaScript.

It's a small meditation on keeping a visual "signature" that changes subtly
each time it's seeded.
