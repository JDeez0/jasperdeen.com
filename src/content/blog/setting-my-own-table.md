This site is a small exercise in doing exactly one thing well: putting **words** down without a dashboard, a CMS, or a feed of what you *should* be writing.

The homepage isn't a landing page — it's a conversation:

> What's going on here?
>
> Making space to write.

That's the whole frame. Everything else is scaffolding.

## Why plain files

The posts here are plain Markdown in the repository. That's a deliberate choice:

- **The file *is* the publish.** No sync layer, no webhook that can silently fail.
- **Zero client JavaScript.** What a reader gets is the text, as HTML, immediately — including for crawlers that never execute scripts.
- **Versioned like code.** Every word goes through git, so nothing is ever lost.
