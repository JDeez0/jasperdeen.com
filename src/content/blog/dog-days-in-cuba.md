The premise is almost too strange to summarize: Benz, a Dobermann puppy, is rescued by a foreign scientist working in Cuba in the late 1990s, nearly executed on the orders of a military officer, and saved when he's recruited as a search dog for a treasure hunt in the caves of Guanahacabibes. He narrates all of it through a "Brain Clone Helmet" — a brain-computer interface built by young Cuban engineers that translates his thoughts into prose. That helmet is the whole trick of the book: it lets a dog see Cuba with total clarity while being too insignificant for anyone to lie to.

Paulina Zelitsky knows whereof she writes. A Ukrainian-born marine engineer who designed ports for Soviet and Cuban companies before defecting in 1971, she was later involved in real underwater treasure expeditions off Cuba's coast. The book is marketed as "a true story" and as a novel based on true events, and she never fully resolves the tension. I stopped trying to resolve it too. The book isn't really about whether the gold of the Cathedral of San Ildefonso de Mérida sat in those caves for 355 years. It's about what a system looks like from underneath — the petty officers, the joint ventures, the state that sent priests to labor camps and then traded a map of hidden treasure to the Vatican for a papal visit.

Calling it satire feels almost generous to the satire, since the cruelty needs no exaggeration. The closest cousin is Animal Farm: an animal narrator doing the work that human witnesses can't safely do. Benz falls in love with a local mongrel named Rubia, and the book ends on that note — *"Extraño a mi amada. No hay justicia, solo una vida de perro en Cuba."* I miss my beloved. There is no justice, only a dog's life in Cuba.

It's a rough book, honestly — self-published, unevenly edited, shelved by Amazon under four different genres because no one, including its author, quite knows what it is. But it's also one of the strangest and most sincere things I've read this year: a defector's testimony wearing a dog's face. Sometimes the naked king is only visible to the animals.
